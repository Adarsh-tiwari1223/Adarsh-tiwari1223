## 3. The 4 Content Pillars That Attract Students & Freshers

### Pillar 1: "What College Doesn't Teach You" (Reality Checks)
- **Topic:** Why academic syllabuses still teach outdated manual concepts while the industry demands Playwright, Docker, and API testing.
- **Example Title:** *"Why you shouldn't waste 6 months learning Selenium in 2026. Start with Playwright instead."*

### Pillar 2: Actionable Step-by-Step Roadmaps
- **Topic:** Concrete 30-day, 60-day, and 90-day learning schedules.
- **Example Title:** *"The 90-Day Roadmap to Go From Zero to SDET in Python (Save this for later)."*
- Break it into: Week 1-3 (Python OOP), Week 4-6 (PyTest & Requests), Week 7-10 (Playwright POM), Week 11-12 (CI/CD GitHub Actions).

### Pillar 3: Resume & Portfolio Fixes
- **Topic:** Critiquing common fresher mistakes.
- **Example Title:** *"Stop putting 'Calculator App' or 'Hospital Management' on your fresher resume. Here is what recruiters actually want to see."*
- Contrast toy projects with enterprise workflows (like building an end-to-end framework like HRlens).

### Pillar 4: Interview Deconstructions & Real Questions
- **Topic:** Practical questions asked in actual technical screening calls.
- **Example Title:** *"3 tricky Playwright interview questions that reject 80% of freshers (and how to answer them)."*

## 4. The "5-Connections-a-Day" Networking Strategy

To build a thriving, engaged network without spamming:

1. **Search for Student Communities**:
   * Search LinkedIn for: `"MCA Student"`, `"B.Tech CSE 2025"`, `"Aspiring SDET"`, `"Job Seeker QA"`.
   * Filter location by: India (Varanasi, Prayagraj, Lucknow, Noida, Delhi NCR, Bangalore).
2. **Send a Supportive Connection Note**:
   ```text
   Hi [Name], I noticed you're currently pursuing your [MCA / B.Tech / Computer Science degree]. As an SDET working in automation, I share weekly practical roadmaps and project advice to help students break into tech. Happy to connect and support your journey! Best, Adarsh.
   ```
3. **Engage in the Comments of Top Educators**:
   * Follow popular QA/SDET creators (e.g., people writing about testing, automation, and fresher careers).
   * Leave thoughtful, multi-sentence advice under questions asked by students in their comment sections. Students will click through to your profile and follow you directly.
